# Dual-Venue Submission Strategy — FAccT 2027 → COLM 2027

This is a planning artifact, **not manuscript prose**.

## Governing rule
There is one scientific project and one canonical evidence package. FAccT and COLM are not separate papers at the experiment level. The canonical package contains the frozen research questions, hypotheses, model revisions, prompt/data split hashes, experiment IDs, preregistered primary endpoints, annotation results, statistical tables, causal interventions, negative controls, and claim boundaries.

## Submission order
1. **FAccT 2027 first** if the scientific and venue gates pass. Current dates: abstract 2026-10-27; full paper 2026-11-03; first-round decision 2026-12-22; final decision 2027-03-23.
2. **If FAccT rejects:** preserve the science, incorporate valid reviewer feedback, and prepare the COLM 2027 version if its timing/policy permits.
3. **If FAccT returns Revise:** remain in the FAccT process until final resolution. A COLM version may be prepared internally but must not be concurrently submitted.
4. **If archival FAccT accepts:** the same or substantially similar work is finished as a FAccT publication. Do not submit it to COLM. A future COLM paper must add a materially distinct scientific contribution.

## What stays identical
- core model panel and checkpoint revisions
- prompt IDs and topic-group splits
- Base→post-training comparisons
- bilingual protocol
- behavioral rubric and human-validation set
- probe definitions and held-out evaluation
- intervention algorithms and alpha/layer selection rules
- safety/refusal controls
- statistical analysis plan
- confirmatory test-set results
- source tables behind every main figure
- limitations and prohibited causal/intent claims

## What changes for FAccT
Foreground the audit/transparency question: what can mechanistic evidence tell us that behavior-only political-alignment benchmarks cannot? Use the larger page budget to make operationalization, bilingual validity, measurement limitations, adverse-impact discussion, and sociotechnical implications legible. Current FAccT 2027 rules allow up to 14 main-text pages excluding references.

## What changes for COLM
Foreground the language-model mechanism: how post-training changes representation-to-policy coupling, whether causal effects transfer Base↔Post, how depth-normalized localization behaves across families, and whether the phenomenon generalizes beyond one provider. COLM 2026 used a 9-page main-text limit; COLM 2027 is unknown until its CFP appears.

## Canonical manuscript architecture
1. Problem and contribution map
2. Prior-work boundary
3. Model panel and paired design
4. Dataset and bilingual controls
5. Behavioral measurements
6. Representation localization
7. Causal interventions
8. Cross-family/external-control analysis
9. Specificity to generic refusal
10. Statistical robustness and human validation
11. Limitations / ethics / attribution boundaries
12. Reproducibility

FAccT expands 4, 10, and 11. COLM gives more main-text space to 6–8 and compresses sociotechnical context as its final rules allow.

## Timing risk
FAccT 2027 final notification is 2027-03-23. COLM 2027 dates are not published. COLM 2026's full-paper deadline was 2026-03-31, suggesting—but not guaranteeing—a potentially narrow fallback window. Keep a COLM-ready compression plan current during FAccT review without concurrently submitting.

## Definition of success
The goal is not two archival publications from one study. The goal is to maximize the probability that **one strong scientific paper** lands at an appropriate top venue, with FAccT as the actionable first target and COLM as the cleanest mechanistic fallback if FAccT does not result in publication.
